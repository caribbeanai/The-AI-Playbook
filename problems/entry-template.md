# Curated entry template

This is an editorial template, not a submission form. Remove placeholders before publication.

- Public ID: a new non-identifying catalogue ID, never the private intake reference
- Title: a specific obstacle and affected activity
- Broad region: omit if location could identify a contributor
- Problem summary: reviewed paraphrase, not raw submission text
- Evidence: public source links and dates, or explicitly “contributor-reported; not independently verified”
- Unknowns: frequency, severity, affected population and other unverified details
- Existing approach: what people currently do, where known
- Simpler option: a non-AI baseline
- Proposed solution: its mechanism, necessary inputs and limitations
- Status: proposed, test planned, tested with documented evidence, or withdrawn
- Test design: public or synthetic inputs, success/failure checks and stopping conditions
- Safeguards: privacy, accessibility, human authority and correction route
- Ownership: authorised organisational role, not an inferred individual assignment
- Publication review: consent checked privately; re-identification risk assessed; reviewer role and date
- Change history: factual revisions and evidence links

Do not publish contact details, exact locations, private submission references, consent records or identifying reviewer information. Store those, if required, in the existing private intake context.
