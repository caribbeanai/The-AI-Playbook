# One Person, One Problem

**Tell us what needs to change. Help shape what gets built.**

The Caribbean AI Association's ambition is to hear one problem from every person in the Caribbean and Latin America, then explore practical solutions. This is a participation goal, not a claim about responses collected or problems solved.

[Share a problem through the existing CAIA intake](https://caribbeanaiassociation.com/problems/). Contributions are reviewed privately. Do not put personal information in a GitHub issue or pull request.

## From a contribution to a proposed solution

1. **Listen privately.** Original submissions stay in the existing CAIA Netlify intake. Optional follow-up contact is not newsletter consent.
2. **Review and protect.** An authorised reviewer checks permission to publish an anonymised summary, removes identifying details and considers re-identification risks in small communities. Unclear consent or sensitive details keep the entry private.
3. **Describe the problem.** State who is affected in general terms, what is difficult, what evidence is available and what remains unknown.
4. **Compare options.** Include a simple non-AI baseline. Artificial intelligence is an option, not a requirement.
5. **Propose a bounded test.** Record an accountable owner, useful outcome, safeguards, success and failure checks, and unresolved dependencies. A proposal is not an approved project.
6. **Report evidence.** Distinguish ideas, tests and demonstrated results. Record limitations and correction history.

## Public register

[Read the machine-readable register](register.json). It starts empty: no submissions or outcomes have been invented. New entries require review and an approved pull request. No automatic import from Netlify is permitted.

Use the [entry template](entry-template.md). Suggested categories include transport, public services, learning, livelihoods, energy, climate and community safety. Categories are organisational aids, not population statistics.

## What never belongs here

Names, email addresses, telephone numbers, precise home locations, submission references, raw free text, health or financial records, identifying allegations, information about children, or confidential organisational material. Removing a name alone does not make a story anonymous. Do not upload private submissions to an AI service for summarisation without separate authorisation.

The public register is not an emergency response service, a representative survey or a guarantee of assistance. Contact details and individual responses remain outside this repository. The form's notice explains its processing and removal route.
