# TurtleBird working records

Copy these into your approved project workspace. Use an evidence reference instead of pasting personal or restricted records. Use synthetic data for anything shared in this public repository.

## Pilot brief

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Service and jurisdiction | The service, operator and public body responsible |
| Goal and baseline | The problem, current staff process and observable outcome |
| Scope | Permitted users, sources, tasks and outputs |
| Human authority | Who checks outputs, who approves release and who can pause use |
| Affected people | Who could face a delay, denial, disclosure or other harm |
| Access and correction | How users reach a person, correct an error or challenge an outcome |
| Fallback | The working manual route, its capacity and the owner of any queue |
| Version and evidence | Model, prompt or configuration, source snapshot and restricted evidence location |

## Test and monitoring window

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Run ID and purpose | Validation, recurring monitoring, incident retest or proposed version change |
| Versions and period | Versions tested, source snapshot and applicable dates |
| Sampling | Selection method, number eligible, number reviewed and limits |
| Planned slices | Test conditions, coverage targets, rationale and review language |
| Scoring rubric | Examples of a pass, an ordinary fail and a critical fail |
| Baseline | How staff complete the same cases and what they are allowed to see |
| Reviewers | Primary and second reviewer, disagreement resolution and evidence location |
| Results | Assessor JSON, meaningful differences, missing evidence and incident references |

## Incident record

Record the time noticed, affected service, detection source and what staff know so far. List any action already taken, who owns it, and the next check-in time. If the service is paused, record the manual route and how queued cases are handled. Keep the evidence and any required notifications in approved channels. Before closing the incident, record the cause or remaining uncertainty, corrective work, retest results and restart authority.

## Release or restart decision

Record **approve within scope**, **hold for evidence** or **stop the pilot**. State the approved scope, decision owner, date, evidence references and open conditions. Include which people or teams need the decision, how staff can suspend use, and when the decision will be revisited. Check who can accept any remaining risk under your organisation's governance.
