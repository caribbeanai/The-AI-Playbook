## Evidence rules for contributions

Label each claim as one of: **proposed pattern**, **existing digital service**, **announced policy/project**, **evaluated pilot**, or **production deployment**. For the latter two, cite the implementing agency, dates, evaluated scope and measured results. Do not turn an announcement into an outcome claim. Vendor marketing alone is insufficient to claim public benefit.

Keep country-specific legal statements dated and linked to the appropriate official authority. Use short summaries and links rather than reproducing protected reports. Retain uncertainty and distinguish a regional reference from a national requirement.
