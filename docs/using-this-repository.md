# Using this repository

[Back to the Playbook](../README.md)

## Five practical uses

1. **Leadership workshop:** choose one ministry area, compare its three levels, and complete the pilot brief for a single service. Record why AI is preferable to simpler process changes.
2. **Frontline training:** use public or synthetic inputs to practise drafting, checking citations and identifying when the model should abstain. Compare output quality and reviewer effort.
3. **Procurement preparation:** turn a selected card's dependencies, risks and metrics into supplier questions and acceptance tests. Have the relevant local procurement authority review the process.
4. **Technical prototype:** load [the JSON catalogue](../government/use-cases.json) to filter cards by area and readiness level. It is reference data, not executable agents or production integrations. Do not connect it to live services without a separately approved design.
5. **Regional knowledge exchange:** contribute an openly documented pilot with methods, limitations and negative results. Keep protected data and confidential implementation details in your agency's own controlled systems.

## Suggested workshop output

One selected use case, named service owner, measured baseline, non-AI alternative, approved data classification, human review point, acceptance thresholds, budget cap, test period, stop conditions and next decision date. Use the [templates](../templates/government-pilot.md).

## Adapt beyond government carefully

Small businesses can adapt approved-document drafting, enquiry routing and aggregate demand planning; schools can adapt teacher-assistive preparation; nonprofits can adapt service-directory and relief-logistics workflows. Their duties, risks and decision rights differ. Do not transplant government legal authority or access permissions into another organisation. The [Playbook website](https://aiplaybooklac.netlify.app/) provides the broader regional context.
