# AI Playbook for the Caribbean and Latin America

A practical public repository for Caribbean and LAC AI adoption: government use cases, local implementation guidance, and reusable responsible-AI templates.

Created by **[Adrian Dunkley](https://adriandunkley.net/)**, physicist and AI scientist, and founder of StarApple AI. Connect with the regional community through the **[Caribbean AI Association](https://caribbeanaiassociation.com/)**.

## Government coverage

The catalogue contains **39 proposed patterns across 13 ministry/function areas**, each spanning **assistive**, **workflow**, and **advanced governed** levels:

1. Finance, revenue and customs
2. Agriculture and food security
3. Climate and disaster management
4. Health and public health
5. Education and training
6. Labour and social protection
7. Tourism, culture and creative industries
8. Transport and public infrastructure
9. Energy, water and utilities
10. Environment, oceans and fisheries
11. Justice and public safety administration
12. Foreign affairs, citizen services and local government
13. Internal HR, procurement and audit

Every card includes value, data, process, tools, a fictional sample output, risks, controls, metrics, dependencies and complexity. These are proposed implementation patterns, **not claims of deployed government systems**. They do not authorise automated benefit denial, clinical decisions, policing profiles, immigration decisions or other consequential decisions about people.

## Caribbean-first implementation

Local languages and dialects, small populations, uneven data, hurricane resilience, low bandwidth, accessibility, public accountability and cross-border data handling are design requirements. Use the [readiness guide](government/caribbean-readiness.md) before choosing a tool. Jurisdictions have different laws and mandates; verify applicable requirements locally.
